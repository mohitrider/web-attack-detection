EVALUATION DESIGN

What to keep in mind

01

Target

Benign is the negative class; the three listed web-attack labels are combined into the positive class for the main detector.

02

Preprocessing

Timestamp and target labels are excluded from predictive features. Non-finite numeric values are handled within training-fitted pipelines.

03

Thresholding

Decision thresholds are selected using validation data, not the opposite-day test labels.

04

Limitations

Only two traffic days and three web-attack categories are studied. Repeated patterns and some conflicting labels were observed and should be disclosed.

i

Source: CSE-CIC-IDS2018, focusing on the Thursday 22-Feb-2018 and Friday 23-Feb-2018 TrafficForML_CICFlowMeter files. Findings apply to this selected subset; they should not be generalised automatically to all networks or attack types.
